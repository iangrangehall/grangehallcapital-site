Acquisition criteria

The box.

SectorsPrecision manufacturing · Technical industrial services
EBITDA$500K to $2M for a platform · Add-ons from $250K
RevenueTypically $3M to $20M
GeographyWisconsin and northern Illinois, within about 90 miles of Milwaukee
OwnershipFounder or family owned, 10+ years in business, owner ready to transition
TransactionFull or majority purchase. SBA 7(a), principal equity and seller financing; equity partners on larger transactions.
HoldPermanent. No fund, no exit date.

Not a fit: retail, restaurants and hospitality · franchises · general contracting and project-only revenue · consumer products · turnarounds · businesses that depend on the owner personally, with no successor in place

Approach

Local equity, not private equity.

Growing founder-built companies with the people who built them.

A generation of Wisconsin founders is ready to step back, and many of their companies have no clear successor. Grange Hall Capital is built to be that successor: a permanent owner that keeps the name, the people and the location, and gives the business what it needs to grow.
